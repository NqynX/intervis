Feature

Hover a gene or attC mark to inspect it. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan; keep zooming to read the DNA sequence.

About this view

Genes sit on a central backbone — forward strand above, reverse below — at their true coordinates, coloured by function. attC recombination sites are marked with an asterisk (✳) above the backbone. Zoom in to read the DNA bases. Click any arrow to pick its colour (alt-click clears it); toggle Region and drag to shade a span in the swatch colour; Reset clears them. Toggle Labels for gene names and export as SVG/PNG/PDF.
