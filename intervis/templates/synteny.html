Hover a gene or attC mark to inspect it, or hover a ribbon for the exact % identity of that orthologous pair. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan; keep zooming to read the DNA bases.

About this view

Each genome is drawn as in the single view — forward strand above, reverse below a backbone, coloured by function, with attC asterisks (✳). Both share one scale, anchored at the integrase, so the gaps are to scale. A ribbon joins a cassette to its reciprocal best hit in the other array, coloured by exact pairwise % identity (or by homology group). Click any arrow to recolour it; toggle Labels for gene names. Hover a ribbon for the number.
